# PDFCompress

**Compress PDFs and photos offline on Windows. No uploads.**

Shrink PDFs and photos on your own Windows PC. Your files are never uploaded, and the free trial needs no account.

- **Website:** https://pdfcompress-site.vercel.app
- **Download (Windows 10/11, 64-bit, v1.0.1, ~113 MB):** [PDFCompress.Setup.1.0.1.exe](https://github.com/SharpGithub0310/pdfcompress-releases/releases/download/v1.0.0/PDFCompress.Setup.1.0.1.exe)
- **For ships and seafarers:** https://pdfcompress-site.vercel.app/ships/

## What it does

- **PDFs:** 3 presets: Maximum (72 DPI), Balanced (150 DPI) and High (200 DPI).
- **Photos:** JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF in; JPEG, PNG or WebP out, with a quality slider.
- **Batch:** pick or drag in many files at once.
- **Safe output:** your original is kept, and the smaller copy is saved as `name_compressed`. If a file can't get smaller, the original is kept.
- **Runs on your PC:** compression happens locally. The internet is only needed once, to activate a paid licence.

What to expect: scans and photo-heavy PDFs shrink the most. Text-only PDFs shrink very little.

1. Download the installer above.
2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** → **Run anyway**. The app is new and independent, and not yet code-signed.
3. Open PDFCompress. The trial starts on its own.
